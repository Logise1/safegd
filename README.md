# SafeGD 🛡️

**SafeGD** is a protective utility designed to clean up your Geometry Dash browsing experience by automatically censoring explicit and inappropriate (+18 / NSFW) levels, particularly within the unmoderated **Recent** tab.

Instead of exposing players to harmful or offensive content, SafeGD catches flagged levels beforehand, masks them safely, and gives full viewing control back to the player.

---

## What SafeGD Does

* **Automatic Level Censoring:** Instantly hides or blurs out flagged levels, preventing explicit artwork, misleading titles, or offensive thumbnails from showing up while browsing.
* **Reveal on Demand:** Provides a toggle option on every censored level card, allowing you to unmask and view the level details if you choose to.
* **Zero Disruption:** Keeps your browsing fast and clean without breaking level browsing or navigation.

---

## Repository Contents

* **`SafeGD`**: The application that handles real-time level censorship and user-controlled reveal toggles.
* **`blacklist.txt`**: A curated database of confirmed inappropriate and malicious level IDs used by the app to identify content that needs to be censored.
